# House AI position review

Reviewed November 13, 2026. This file is a work log. It is not part of the site.

Each entry lists what the member's own public statements and tracked bill actions show, the rows changed, the quotes added with their source and date, and the resulting position. Quotes were checked against the cited page, and dates come from the page. A page with no date is marked undated and is used only when the member has no dated source. Quotes are used only when the page attributes them to the member.

Positions use the ranking in `notes/about-map.md`: stronger-risk regulation, then opposing regulation or light-touch only, then AI ethical concerns, then unknown. A member who prioritizes racing China ahead of AI guardrails counts as opposing regulation even when they acknowledge existential risk. A member who wants to stay ahead of China and also wants a treaty with China on AI risk counts as supporting stronger-risk regulation.
